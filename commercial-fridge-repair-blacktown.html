Signs Your Commercial Fridge Needs Repair

Catching a fault early can save you thousands in lost stock and a more expensive repair later. Call a commercial refrigeration mechanic in Blacktown if you notice any of these warning signs:

Temperature Creeping Up

The display reads above 5°C, or products feel warmer than usual.

Ice Build-Up

Frost on the evaporator coil, back wall or freezer floor.

Water Leaking

Pooling under the unit or inside the cabinet.

Constant Running

The compressor never switches off.

Strange Noises

Clicking, buzzing, rattling or grinding from the motor or fans.

Door Seals Not Closing

Torn, loose or gapped door gaskets.

Error Codes or Blank Display

Controller faults on digital units.

Higher Power Bills

A sign your system is working harder than it should.

Refrigeration Maintenance: Prevent Breakdowns Before They Happen

Most commercial fridge breakdowns are caused by problems that regular servicing would catch early, like dirty condenser coils, worn door seals and blocked drains.

Our refrigeration maintenance plans include:

  • Cleaning condenser and evaporator coils
  • Checking refrigerant pressures and electrical connections
  • Testing thermostats, controllers and fans
  • Inspecting door seals and hinges
  • Clearing drain lines
  • A written service report for your records

Regular commercial refrigeration service helps lower power bills, extend equipment life and keep your business compliant with food safety requirements. Most busy kitchens benefit from a service every 3 to 6 months.

Protect Your Stock and Stay Food-Safe

Under Australia's Food Standards Code, potentially hazardous food must be stored at 5°C or below, and frozen food must stay frozen. A failing fridge or freezer can put you at risk of stock losses and a poor result in your next council food safety inspection.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does commercial fridge repair cost in Blacktown?

The cost depends on the equipment, the fault and the parts needed. Our call-out fee is $69, and we always give you an upfront quote before starting any repair.

Do you offer same-day commercial fridge repairs?

Yes. We offer same-day service across Blacktown and Western Sydney, and 24/7 emergency call-outs for urgent breakdowns.

Are your technicians licensed?

Yes. Our refrigeration mechanics hold an ARC refrigerant handling licence, which is required by law for work involving refrigerant gas.

Do you repair cool rooms and freezer rooms?

Yes. We repair walk-in cool rooms and freezer rooms, including evaporators, condensing units, door seals and controllers.

Should I repair or replace my commercial fridge?

If the unit is less than 8 to 10 years old and the repair is a fraction of the replacement cost, repairing usually makes sense. We'll give you honest advice either way.

How often should commercial refrigeration be serviced?

We recommend a professional service every 3 to 6 months for busy kitchens, and at least once a year for lighter use.

What should I do while waiting for a technician?

Keep doors closed, move high-risk food to a working unit, and check temperatures with a probe thermometer. Don't keep restarting the unit, as this can make some faults worse.
